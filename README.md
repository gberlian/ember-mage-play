# Ember Mage, playable build

The browser build of Ember Mage, an original third-person wizard game (Rust and Bevy, WebGL2).

- Play: https://7flash.github.io/ember-mage-play/
- Link to share on X (a player card that plays in the post): https://7flash.github.io/ember-mage-play/card.html

Controls: click to capture the mouse, WASD to move, mouse to aim, left and right buttons and Q, F, E to cast. In a frame that can't capture the mouse, hold a button or a finger and drag to look.

Models are CC0 packs by Kay Lousberg (KayKit) and Kenney; their licence files are under `assets/`.
